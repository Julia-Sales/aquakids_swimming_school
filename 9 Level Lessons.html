9 Level Lessons

Progressive levels to build confidence and skill in the pool. Click any lesson to learn more.

SEAL
Level 1

Introductory session focused on comfort in water, blowing bubbles and floating basics.

CLOWNFISH
Level 2

Controlled breathing, submerging safely and returning to the surface.

OCTOPUS
Level 3

Back and front floats with basic kicking to move through the water.

PUFFER FISH
Level 4

Introduce coordinated arm strokes, body alignment and streamline glides.

SEAHORSE
Level 5

Combine arms and kicks with rhythmic breathing for longer swims.

RAY
Level 6

Build endurance and introduce safe wall turns and transitions.

TURTLE
Level 7

Refine stroke technique and increase speed with targeted drills.

DOLPHIN
Level 8

Advanced drills: starts, dives (where appropriate) and efficiency over distance.

SHARK
Level 9

Polish all skills and practice sets that combine technique, speed and stamina.
